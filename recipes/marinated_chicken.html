Marinated Chicken

Description

Chicken is a healthy, light, and easy-to-cook food, perfect as a main course and as a Sunday family meal, especially when it comes to oven-baked chicken with potatoes. However, being a white meat like turkey, it tends to dry out during cooking, especially when baked or grilled. On the other hand, dishes such as Chicken Cacciatore or Chicken with Peppers, both of which have a rich sauce, are naturally tender and do not require marinating.

Therefore, marinating is recommended if you want to cook chicken in the oven, in a pan, or on the grill. Even when using chicken breast, one trick to keep it tender and juicy is to marinate it.

Ingredients

  • 600g chicken
  • 50 ml lemon juice (you can also use wine or beer)
  • 50 ml extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh herbs (to taste)
  • Dried oregano (or spices to taste)
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Take the chicken pieces and season them with salt. Massage them with your hands so that the salt penetrates the meat thoroughly.
  2. Add the olive oil, lemon juice, and oregano. Mix everything well with your hands, then cover with an airtight lid and place the marinated chicken in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours.
  3. As mentioned earlier, these are the ingredients I used, but you can also choose rosemary, thyme, paprika, pepper, chili pepper, garlic, and many other ingredients of your choice to make the chicken more flavorful. As for the liquids, white or red wine, vinegar, beer, soy sauce, coconut milk, etc. work very well. It always depends on how you intend to cook the chicken.
  4. After letting the marinated chicken rest, heat the grill and place the chicken pieces on it. If you're using chicken breasts, 3–4 minutes per side should be enough. If you're using larger chicken pieces, I recommend cooking them with a weight on top, such as a pot filled with water, for about 30 minutes.
    • OVEN-BAKED: Place the chicken together with all of the marinade in a baking dish and cook in a conventional oven at 200°C (390°F) for about 45 minutes, turning the chicken from time to time.
    • PAN-FRIED: Place the chicken together with all of the marinade in a pan and cook for about 30-35 minutes with the lid on, turning the chicken from time to time.
